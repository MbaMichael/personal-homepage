Hello, I'm

Michael Mba

Originally, I was stressing about what I would write, so I started with someone other than myself. If you continue below, you'll find out who. So, what did that do for me? Absolutely nothing, but it bought me time to reflect a little more while I considered the writing style for this project. Definitely more humorous and a little unprofessional, so I hope you don't mind. Anyways, I've delayed long enough.

Hello to whoever is reading this. As you can infer, my name is Michael Mba, an aspiring engineer and passionate nerd. I was raised in Nigeria for 11 years, and educated in the US for another 11 years, making me an eccentric 22-year-old. I have come to learn and appreciate much about my upbringing as I was a very sheltered child, but somedays I miss the simplicity of things. So I look forward to all that I can and will be.

Some interests of mine include tabletop (Dungeons and Dragons, Magic the Gathering, etc.), writing (currently working on a science fantasy novel), and the arts (I have no particular attachment to any artform, and I like to dabble). I consider myself a techie, keeping up with the latest tech news as much as I can and I enjoy the continuous process of DIY projects. It's no wonder I ended up liking a certain Marvel character, huh?

"Hell answers to me. For I am DOOM."

I have a thing for the edgy yet complex characters, and Doctor Doom is the embodiment of that fascination because of how much he blurs the line between hero and villain.

This fascination has bled into my other hobbies that involve Marvel characters, especially when the creative capacity for displaying this dynamism is at its peak. What am I talking about? Well recently, I have taken up to Magic the Gathering and they released a new Marvel crossover set, with a few cards featuring Doctor Doom. So, as a resident Doom fan, I had to try my hand at creating a deck for the Latverian Monarch. If you're into Magic the Gathering, check it out and let me know what you think! It's currently a work-in-progress deck.
